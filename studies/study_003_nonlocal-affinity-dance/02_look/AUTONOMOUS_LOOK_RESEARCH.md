# Autonomous Look research — closed

The former autonomous Look-direction experiment ran through Look rounds 001–008 and was closed on 2026-08-20. Its generated workspaces were removed from the active Study on 2026-08-21 when the Look phase was flattened for artist ownership.

## Conclusion

The experiment built strong technical safeguards but did not produce publication-quality visual work. Round 008's Collective Skin worker made 90 API calls and reported 15,953,632 total tokens, including 354,499 input, 40,477 output, and 15,558,656 cache-read tokens. It exceeded the configured 200,000-token acceptance ceiling and was frozen as failed research provenance. No retry, comparison, promotion, or human approval was granted.

The central failure was architectural: an autonomous worker owned concept interpretation, Houdini construction, materials, lighting, framing, rendering, measurements, evidence, and receipts before KC could judge the first useful image. Better schemas and provenance improved technical reliability but did not create better taste or faster visual convergence.

## Retained reusable work

System-level code and documentation retain optional downstream mechanisms for:

- canonical Behavior/cache binding;
- deterministic Hython scene setup;
- fresh-reopen source, graph, MaterialX/USD, camera, light, and render checks;
- bounded Windows process-tree execution;
- resumable rendering and media validation;
- usage accounting and retry limits;
- locked-scene checksums and render receipts.

These mechanisms may support setup or rendering. They are not an autonomous Look pipeline and must not modify an artist-authored locked HIP.

## Replacement workflow

The active workflow is artist-led:

1. KC specifies a starter HIP in a short brainstorm.
2. Hermes copies and binds a jointly curated setup.
3. KC completes Look Development in the flat `02_look` directory, including colour and framing.
4. KC identifies and locks the authoritative HIP.
5. Hermes performs bounded rendering without aesthetic changes.
6. The verified render goes directly to Specimen/detail work.
7. Automation is considered only after repeated completed works expose stable patterns.
